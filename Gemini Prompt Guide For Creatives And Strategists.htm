Why are prompts important?

A prompt is an instruction you write to interact with a chatbot.

Large Language Models (LLM) can make sense of prompts written in a conversational tone. Natural Language Processing (NLP) is a significant development in improving AI systems.

When you chat with Gemini, the prompts you write play a key role in shaping the response. Thoughtful prompts lead to better results.

Next, we explore a structure to increase the quality of your prompts.

The Art of the Prompt The Building Blocks 


Persona

What role do you want Gemini to take on?

You are a Content Marketer for a major travel company.

 

Task

Clearly define the task and/or goal.

Generate 5 ideas for blog content that feel relevant and will appeal to frequent travelers and the tourism industry.

Context

Provide more context on  the style or content.

Make the topics unique. There are lots of tourism blogs out there — let’s come up with fresh angles that would stand out. Keep the topics relevant. Tap into current trends or recent challenges and or innovations within the tourism industry when brainstorming.

Format

What format do you want the result to take?

Share back the ideas in a bulleted list. Each idea should include a section for a) the target audience and why this appeals to them. b) The content outline c) a CTA.


The Art of the Prompt The Building Blocks 

You don’t always need to include all four; but it can be a good mental model for yourself when prompting. It can get a little getting used to; but imagine you’re talking to a person with none of the context you have.

What am I really asking for?

Am I expecting a certain style?

And just for fun; what if I asked for the complete opposite?

Tips on Getting 

            Started    …

By now, you’ve hopefully got an insight into the breadth of tasks and situations where Gemini can support the strategy and creative process. But the real unlock is to find how Gemini can help your work and routine. Before we leave, here are a few ideas on how to get started. Oh, and they all should take less than 5 minutes….

Bring AI Along for the Ride

The real key to unlocking the potential of Gemini and AI is finding use cases that really help you in your day-to-day work; whether it’s time and productivity savings, generating new ideas and perspectives, all the way to tackling projects that might have seen too overwhelming or difficult before. Here are a few steps on how to do that.

Takeaway One:

Head to your Google Drive (or Google Docs/Sheets) and take a look at the last 10 documents you worked on. Pick one or two and revisit; only now through the lens of Gemini. How might Gemini have helped you with this document? Could it have helped brainstorm the contents or agenda? Analyze a report or data source? Polish the writing?

01

At the beginning of the presentation, we also touched upon the benefits of creating a culture around AI experimentation across your team. Here are a few quick and easy wins to put that into practice:

Takeaway Two:

Consider using 5 minutes at the beginning or end of a weekly team meeting to talk about AI and Gemini. Maybe it’s an opportunity to share interesting use cases (from work or personal life) or an opportunity to discuss interesting inspiration examples from the industry.

02

Takeaway Three:

Why not start a ‘prompt library’ for your team? It could be as simple as a Google Doc or Sheet where your teammates can share interesting use cases and the prompts they used as they discover them. It’s a great way of discovering new ideas and approaches.

03

Has your team stumbled upon a great use case or prompt? Consider turning it into a Gem! Gems are custom versions of Gemini that can be created to tackle specific tasks. For example; a ‘Brainstorming Assistant’ or a ‘Report Analyzer’. They’re incredibly quick and easy to make; here are a few steps.

Takeaway Four:

1.             Navigate to ‘Gem Manager’ in the left-hand navigation bar of Gemini.

2.             Click ‘New Gem’

3.             Give your Gem a name based on the task you’re developing.

4.             Add the instructions (your prompt) for the Gem.

5.             Give it a whirl! Once your happy with the output; you’ll be able to return to this Gem whenever you want without having to re-prompt.

More Than Words

We’ve also covered Gemini’s multi-modal abilities. Some of the most interesting use cases involve supplementing a text prompt with another form of media (for example, an image or a document). Don’t forget, it can generate images too!

Takeaway Five:

Try one interesting use case that leverages Gemini’s multi-modal features. Perhaps it’s sharing an infographic you discovered in a report and asking for an analysis or some follow-ups. Or maybe it’s a photo of your bookcase with an ask for suggestions on new books based on your reading habits?
